Global Strategy | Positioning for a soft landing

We move Overweight equities and trim duration. With core PCE trending back toward target and the labor market cooling without cracking, we think the balance of risks now favors a soft landing, and we expect the Fed to deliver two 25bp cuts before year-end. This should support a broadening of the equity rally beyond the mega-cap names that have driven index returns.

We rotate out of defensives and into cyclicals and small caps, which have historically outperformed as the yield curve dis-inverts. We remain Underweight long-duration Treasuries — while we see the hiking cycle as over, we think the market has priced in too many cuts, and term premium is likely to rise as fiscal supply stays heavy.

Risks to our view. A resurgence in inflation would force a hawkish repricing and is the key risk to our risk-on stance. A hard landing, though not our base case, would argue for the opposite trade: long duration, defensives over cyclicals. We would turn more cautious if initial jobless claims break decisively above 260k.
