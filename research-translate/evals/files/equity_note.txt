Aurora Semiconductor (ASX.O) | Initiating at Buy | PT $145 (28% upside)

We initiate coverage of Aurora Semiconductor at Buy with a 12-month price target of $145, implying 28% upside from current levels. Aurora is well-positioned to benefit from the secular ramp in AI accelerator demand, and we believe consensus is underestimating the durability of its gross margin expansion.

Investment thesis. Aurora's advanced packaging capacity is effectively sold out through FY26, giving the company meaningful pricing power. We forecast revenue CAGR of 34% over FY25-27 and see gross margin expanding ~450bps to 61% by FY27 as mix shifts toward higher-ASP HBM-adjacent products. Management guided FY25 revenue up at the last print, and we sit ~6% above the Street on FY26 EPS.

Catalysts. We see three near-term catalysts: (1) the Q3 print in late October, where we expect a modest beat and a guide-up; (2) a potential second large hyperscaler customer, which could add $1.2bn to the FY26 revenue base; and (3) capacity expansion updates at the November analyst day.

Key risks. A sharp correction in AI capex would be a material headwind. We are also cautious on the concentration risk, as the top two customers account for ~55% of revenue. A faster-than-expected de-rating of the broader semis complex could weigh on the multiple even if fundamentals hold.

Valuation. Our $145 PT is based on 32x our FY26 EPS estimate of $4.53, a premium to the peer group that we think is justified by Aurora's superior growth and returns profile (ROIC of 28% vs. peers at ~19%). On EV/EBITDA, the stock trades at 18x NTM, roughly in line with high-growth semi peers.
